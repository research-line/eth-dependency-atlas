# Pilot report (2026-11-06)

- **Sample:** 50 of the 360 frame works with abstracts (seed 20261106). Types: 20 journal articles, 17 conference papers, 10 preprints, 2 theses, 1 book; years 2011–2025.
- **Classifiers:** A = Claude Sonnet (Claude Code subagent, 203,512 tokens); G = Gemini 4 Argon (Medium) via Antigravity with an explicit `--model`. A third run without `--model` turned out to use Claude Sonnet 5.5 (the app default) and is kept only as a non-independent supplement. Codex (usage limit) and Kimi (account suspended) were unavailable.
- **Quality:** Verbatim quotes 82/82 (A) and 81/81 (G). Agreement A–G: 86 % exact, Cohen's κ = 0.75, 88 % coarse (affected / not affected / unclear).
- **Result after adjudication:**
  - 37/50 affected, 74 % (95 % Wilson interval 60.4–84.1 %).
  - Of these, 33 unsupported, 2 rescuable (P05, P41) and 2 refuted candidates (P07, P45).
  - 9 not affected, 4 unclear; 10 need the full text.
- **Lessons:** Most hits are parameterized bounds (2^{o(k)}, n^{o(k)}, 2^{o(√n)}, n^{2−ε}) and only unsupported. A direct refutation requires an explicit linear encoding, which classifiers rarely attempt unprompted; brief v2 makes this mandatory. Size measures are often ambiguous (|G| as vertex count or as input size).
